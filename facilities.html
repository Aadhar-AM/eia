Professional-grade indoor basketball court with wooden flooring and proper lighting for evening practice sessions.

Temperature-controlled Olympic-sized swimming pool with separate lanes for training and competition.

FIFA-standard football field with professional turf and proper drainage system.

Professional cricket ground with practice nets and proper pitch maintenance facilities.

Well-equipped physics lab with modern instruments for experiments in mechanics, electricity, and optics.

State-of-the-art chemistry lab with fume hoods, safety equipment, and all necessary apparatus for practical experiments.

Modern auditorium with advanced sound and lighting systems for performances and events.

Conveniently located bus stops throughout the city with proper shelter and security measures.
