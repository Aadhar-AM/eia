Our Achievements

Celebrating Excellence in Education

Celebrating Success

At Eklavya International Academy, we take pride in the numerous achievements of our students, teachers, and the institution as a whole. These accomplishments reflect our commitment to excellence in education and holistic development.

Our students consistently excel in academic competitions, sports events, cultural programs, and various other activities, bringing laurels to our school and making us proud.

Academic Achievements

Academic Excellence Award

2023

Recognized as one of the top 10 schools in the region for academic performance by the State Education Board.

Science Olympiad Winners

2023

Our students secured 5 gold medals, 7 silver medals, and 3 bronze medals in the National Science Olympiad.

Outstanding Board Exam Results

2023

100% pass rate with 85% of students scoring above 90% in the CBSE Board Examinations.

Sports Achievements

Inter-School Sports Championship

2023

Winners of the Inter-School Sports Championship for the third consecutive year, securing 15 gold, 12 silver, and 8 bronze medals.

Basketball Team Achievement

2023

Our senior basketball team won the State Championship and represented the state at the National Level.

Swimming Competition

2023

Our students won 8 medals in the District Swimming Competition, including 4 gold medals.

Cultural Achievements

Dance Competition

2023

First prize in the Inter-School Dance Competition for both classical and contemporary dance categories.

Music Festival

2023

Our school choir won the Best Choir Award at the State Music Festival for the second year in a row.

Drama Competition

2023

First prize in the Inter-School Drama Competition for our production of "The Merchant of Venice".

Other Achievements

Environmental Initiative

2023

Recognized as a Green School by the Environmental Protection Agency for our sustainable practices and initiatives.

Community Service

2023

Awarded for outstanding community service initiatives, including blood donation camps and educational outreach programs.

Innovation Award

2023

Our students won the National Innovation Award for developing a sustainable water purification system.
