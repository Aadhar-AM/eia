Our Faculty

Meet the dedicated educators who inspire and guide our students

Excellence in Education

At Eklavya International Academy, our faculty members are the cornerstone of our educational excellence. With diverse backgrounds, extensive experience, and a passion for teaching, our educators are committed to nurturing the potential of every student.

Our teachers bring an average of 15+ years of experience in education, with many holding advanced degrees and specialized certifications in their respective fields.

Dr. Rajesh Kumar

Principal

Ph.D. in Educational Leadership

With over 25 years of experience in education, Dr. Kumar has led several prestigious institutions. His vision for Eklavya International Academy is to create an environment where every student can thrive.
